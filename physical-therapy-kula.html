01 / SERVING UPCOUNTRY

We bring the clinic to your door.

Island PT is a mobile, in-home practice, and Kula is part of our Upcountry Maui service area. Instead of asking you to make the drive down the mountain to a clinic, Teddy comes to you, equipment and all, and works with you right where you live on the slopes of Haleakalā. See how our mobile physical therapy visits work.

That matters more than it sounds. Up here, the real challenges show up in your own environment, the slope of your driveway, the steps into the house, the way you move around the garden or the barn. Seeing that first-hand is how we build a plan that actually fits your daily life.

02 / WHO WE HELP IN KULA

For an Upcountry life.

Kula living is hands-on and active, hiking the cooler trails near Haleakalā, tending gardens and farms in the rich Upcountry soil, managing property, and staying on your feet across hilly, uneven ground.

A lot of what we treat up here comes from that lifestyle, low back pain from bending and lifting in the garden, knees and hips from hiking and working on slopes, rotator cuff and shoulder strain from property and ranch work. We also help residents who want to hold onto their balance, strength, and independence on terrain that does not forgive a stumble.

Whatever brought you to us, the approach is the same: take the time to understand the root cause of the problem, then build you back so you can keep doing the things Upcountry life is built around.

Common reasons Kula calls
  • Low back pain from gardening and lifting
  • Knee and hip strain from hiking and slopes
  • Rotator cuff and shoulder wear from ranch work
  • Balance and stability on uneven ground
  • Staying strong and independent with age

Common focus areas: Low back pain · Knee osteoarthritis · Hip pain · Rotator cuff injury · Achilles and calf strain · Balance training

03 / WHY KULA

Care that fits the mountain.

Fewer, longer, more focused visits, brought up the mountain to you.

Getting to quality physical therapy from Upcountry usually means a long drive down and back, over and over, for appointments that never seem to wrap up. Island PT flips that. We come to you, and we build a plan around fewer, longer, more focused visits that are designed to finish.

You get the full attention of a Doctor of Physical Therapy for the whole session, on a schedule that works around your day rather than eating half of it in traffic on the way to the coast.

What that means in practice
  • We travel Upcountry to Kula, no drive down for you
  • One-to-one with a Doctor of Physical Therapy, every visit
  • Focused 6 to 8 week treatment plans designed to finish
  • Virtual consultations available between visits

04 / MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Care from a Doctor of Physical Therapy.

Every visit in Kula is one-to-one with Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and Board Certified Clinical Specialist in Orthopaedics through the ABPTS. He earned his doctorate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and is licensed in Hawaii (PT-4524).

His clinical training spans the McKenzie Method, the Selective Functional Movement Assessment, the Functional Movement Screen, the BackFitPro McGill Method for low back pain, Blood Flow Restriction training, and custom foot orthotic fabrication. That range is what lets him find the root cause instead of chasing symptoms.

05 / OUR SPECIALTIES

What we help with.
