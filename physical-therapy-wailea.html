For the golf, the court, and the years ahead.

Wailea life is active in a particular way, mornings on the course at Wailea or Makena, afternoons on the tennis court, and a strong pull to stay independent and capable well into your later decades.

04 / MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Care from a Doctor of Physical Therapy.

Every visit in Wailea is one-to-one with Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and Board Certified Clinical Specialist in Orthopaedics through the ABPTS. He earned his doctorate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and is licensed in Hawaii (PT-4524).

His clinical training spans the McKenzie Method, the Selective Functional Movement Assessment, the Functional Movement Screen, the BackFitPro McGill Method for low back pain, Blood Flow Restriction training, and custom foot orthotic fabrication. That range is what lets him find the root cause instead of chasing symptoms.

FAQ

Questions from Wailea.

Common questions from Wailea patients about concierge visits, golf and tennis injuries, and recovery.

Do you come to my home in Wailea?

Yes. Island PT is a concierge, in-home practice based just north in Kīhei, a short drive from Wailea. Teddy brings everything needed to your home or resort residence, so there is no clinic to travel to and no waiting room.

Can you help with golf and tennis injuries?

Yes. A lot of what we see in Wailea comes from golf and tennis, low backs, hips, rotator cuff, and tennis elbow. We look at your whole movement pattern, not just the painful joint, so the fix holds up round after round.

Do you handle post-surgical recovery?

Yes. We work with patients recovering from total knee and hip replacements and other procedures, guiding rehab step by step so you return to golf, tennis, and daily life on solid footing rather than rushing it.

Why cash-pay instead of insurance?

Cash-pay is what lets us give you long, unhurried, one-to-one visits without billing quotas dictating your care. For patients who value their time and want results in fewer visits, it tends to be the better investment. Our Why Cash-Pay page explains the model in full.
