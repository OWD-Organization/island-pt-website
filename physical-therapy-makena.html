01 / IN-HOME CARE IN MĀKENA

The visit comes to Mākena.

Mākena sits on the South Maui coast, just south of Wailea. Island PT is a mobile practice based in Kīhei, so rather than asking you to drive to a clinic, Teddy travels to your home with the equipment each session needs. Read more about how in-home physical therapy works.

There is no waiting room and no hand-off to an aide. You work with one clinician, Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, from the first assessment through the last progression of your home program.

02 / WHO WE SEE IN MĀKENA

Ocean days, coastal walks, and a body that keeps up.

Life down here tends to happen outdoors: a swim before the heat of the day, a long walk on the sand, time in the garden or out on the water.

When something starts to hurt, it often shows up in the shoulders, the low back, the hips, or the knees. Sometimes it is an old injury that never quite settled. Sometimes it is a joint replacement that needs proper rehab, or a sense that your balance is not what it used to be.

We assess how you move as a whole, find what is actually driving the problem, and leave you with a plan you can keep working on between visits.

What brings Mākena patients to us
  • Shoulder pain from swimming and paddling
  • Low back and hip pain that cuts walks short
  • Rehab after knee or hip replacement and other surgery
  • Balance work and fall prevention
  • Rebuilding strength after time away from activity

Common focus areas: Shoulder pain · Low back pain · Post-surgical rehab · Balance training · Osteoarthritis

03 / GETTING STARTED

Start when you are ready.

In Hawaiʻi, physical therapy is direct access, so you can book without a referral.

If your doctor has already suggested physical therapy, or you simply know it is time to deal with a nagging problem, you can reach out to us directly. If you plan to seek out-of-network reimbursement, check whether your insurance plan asks for a referral.

Start with a free discovery call. Tell us what is going on, and we will talk through whether in-home care is the right next step for you.

How care is set up
  • Visits at your home, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM
  • A first evaluation of about 90 to 120 minutes
  • A focused 6 to 8 week plan built to finish
  • Virtual consultations when an in-person visit is not practical

04 / YOUR THERAPIST

Meet Teddy Zabel.

Teddy holds a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and is board certified through the ABPTS as an Orthopaedic Clinical Specialist (OCS). He is licensed to practice in Hawaiʻi under license PT-4524.

His assessments draw on the McKenzie Method, the Selective Functional Movement Assessment, the Functional Movement Screen, and the BackFitPro McGill Method for back pain. When a case calls for it, he also uses Blood Flow Restriction training and fabricates custom foot orthotics. Read Teddy's full background.

05 / CORE CARE

Three areas we focus on.

The same three pillars guide care in Mākena as everywhere else we visit.

Spine & Back Care.

Relief for persistent back and neck pain, built on a clear diagnosis and exercises you can keep doing on your own.

Mobility & Balance.

Work on joint range, footing, and steadiness so beach walks and uneven ground feel secure again.

Strength-Based Therapy.

Progressive loading that rebuilds capacity for swimming, paddling, and everyday lifting.
