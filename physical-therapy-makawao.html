01 / SERVING PANIOLO COUNTRY

We bring the clinic to your door.

Makawao is the hub of Upcountry Maui, a paniolo town on the slopes of Haleakalā where ranchers, farmers, and artists have kept a tight-knit community going for generations. Island PT is a mobile, in-home practice, and Makawao is part of our Upcountry service area, so instead of asking you to drive 30 or 40 minutes down to the coast, Teddy comes to you.

He brings the treatment table and equipment right to your home, barn, or studio, and works with you where you actually live and move. Up here, that is the difference between guessing at a problem and seeing it, how you mount up, how you lift a feed bag, how you move around your land. Here is more on how physical therapy at home works with Island PT.

02 / WHO WE HELP IN MAKAWAO

Built for a working Upcountry.

Makawao life is physical in a way the coast is not, horseback riding and ranch work, farming the rich Upcountry soil, hiking the Makawao Forest Reserve, and the daily demands of keeping land and animals.

A lot of what we treat here comes from that world, low back pain from riding and lifting, rotator cuff and shoulder strain from ranch and property work, knees and hips from hiking and working on slopes. We also see the town's artists and makers with neck, shoulder, and wrist strain from long hours at the bench, plus residents focused on keeping their balance and strength as the years go on.

Whatever brought you in, the approach is the same: take the time to find the root cause, then build you back so you can keep riding, working, and doing the things Makawao is built around.

Common reasons Makawao calls
  • Low back pain from riding and lifting
  • Rotator cuff and shoulder strain from ranch work
  • Knee and hip strain from hiking and slopes
  • Neck, shoulder, and wrist strain from studio work
  • Balance and independence on uneven ground

Common focus areas: Low back pain · Rotator cuff injury · Equestrian and riding strain · Knee osteoarthritis · Tendinopathy · Balance training

03 / WHY MAKAWAO

Care that fits the mountain.

Fewer, longer, more focused visits, brought up the mountain to you.

Reaching quality physical therapy from Makawao usually means the long haul down to Kahului or the coast, again and again, for appointments that never seem to finish. Island PT flips that. We come up to you, and we build a plan around fewer, longer, more focused visits designed to actually wrap up.

You get the full attention of a Doctor of Physical Therapy for the whole session, at your place in town or out on the ranch, on a schedule that works around your day instead of eating it in traffic down the mountain.

What that means in practice
  • We travel Upcountry to Makawao, no drive down for you
  • One-to-one with a Doctor of Physical Therapy, every visit
  • Focused 6 to 8 week treatment plans designed to finish
  • Virtual consultations available between visits

04 / MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Care from a Doctor of Physical Therapy.

Every visit in Makawao is one-to-one with Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and Board Certified Clinical Specialist in Orthopaedics through the ABPTS. He earned his doctorate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and is licensed in Hawaii (PT-4524).

His clinical training spans the McKenzie Method, the Selective Functional Movement Assessment, the Functional Movement Screen, the BackFitPro McGill Method for low back pain, Blood Flow Restriction training, and custom foot orthotic fabrication. That range is what lets him find the root cause instead of chasing symptoms.

05 / OUR SPECIALTIES

What we help with.

Wherever in Makawao you are coming from, our core care areas stay the same.

Spine & Back Care.

Evidence-based treatment for chronic back pain, disc issues, and sciatica, the strain that builds up from riding, lifting, and working your land.

Mobility & Balance.

Structured rehab that restores range of motion, builds stability, and reduces fall risk on the hilly, uneven ground of paniolo country.

Strength-Based Therapy.

Gentle, progressive strengthening that keeps you capable in the saddle, on the trail, in the studio, and everywhere Upcountry life takes you.
