01 / LOCALLY ROOTED IN KĪHEI

Care that comes to you.

Kīhei is home for Island PT. It is where the practice is based and where most of our in-home visits happen, right in your living room, your garage gym, or wherever you actually train and recover along the South Maui coast. Teddy brings the treatment table and equipment to you, so there is no clinic to drive to and no waiting room to sit in.

What that means is simple: you get his full attention for the whole visit. No handing you off to an aide halfway through, no watching the clock. Just one Doctor of Physical Therapy, one plan, built around how you live between Kalama Park, Kamaole Beach, and the rest of the south side.

02 / WHO WE HELP IN KĪHEI

Built for an active south side.

Kīhei moves. Daily ocean swims, stand-up paddleboarding before work, surf sessions, long walks along Sugar Beach, and a year-round outdoor life that puts real, repeated demand on your body.

We help Kīhei residents with the injuries that come from staying active, rotator cuff and shoulder pain from paddling, low back pain and lumbar strain from surfing, hip and knee wear from being on your feet all day. We also work with people managing chronic back pain, recovering after surgery, or rebuilding balance and strength so they can keep doing what they love into their 40s, 50s, 60s, and beyond.

Whether you have lived here for decades or just moved to the south side, the goal is the same: get to the root cause of the problem, then build you back stronger than the injury found you.

  • Hip and knee wear from staying active
  • Post-surgical and post-operative recovery
  • Balance, stability, and fall prevention

04 / MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Care from a Doctor of Physical Therapy.

Every visit in Kīhei is one-to-one with Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and Board Certified Clinical Specialist in Orthopaedics through the ABPTS. He earned his doctorate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and is licensed in Hawaii (PT-4524).

His clinical training spans the McKenzie Method, the Selective Functional Movement Assessment, the Functional Movement Screen, the BackFitPro McGill Method for low back pain, Blood Flow Restriction training, and custom foot orthotic fabrication. That range is what lets him find the root cause instead of chasing symptoms.

Yes. Island PT is a mobile, in-home practice based in Kīhei. Teddy brings the equipment to you and works with you in the space where you actually live and move, which is where a lot of the real problems show up.
