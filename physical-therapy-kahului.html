01 / MOBILE PT IN KAHULUI

Skip the trip to a clinic.

Kahului is in Central Maui, on the low ground between the West Maui Mountains and the slopes of Haleakalā. Island PT is a mobile practice, so instead of fitting clinic appointments around shifts and school schedules, you are seen at home. See how mobile physical therapy works from start to finish.

Each appointment is with Teddy Zabel, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and board-certified orthopaedic specialist. The person who evaluates you is the person who treats you, visit after visit.

02 / WHO WE HELP IN KAHULUI

Busy weeks, active weekends, and the aches in between.

Central Maui keeps people moving: long shifts, school runs, yard work, and weekends spent running, playing, or at the beach.

That mix produces the problems we see most often: a low back that tightens up after a day of lifting or sitting, a knee or ankle that has not felt right since a sports injury, a neck and shoulders that carry the stress of the week.

We also guide people through recovery after surgery, and help runners who want a clearer picture of their mechanics through our RunEasi running assessment.

Common reasons people in Kahului reach out
  • Low back and neck pain from work or long days
  • Sports and running injuries
  • Recovery after orthopedic surgery
  • Ankle, knee, and hip pain that keeps coming back
  • Building strength to stay active

Common focus areas: Low back pain · Running injuries · Post-surgical rehab · Knee and ankle pain · Strength training

03 / HOW CARE WORKS

A plan with a finish line.

Longer, focused sessions and a defined plan, instead of an open-ended run of short appointments.

Your first evaluation runs about 90 to 120 minutes, so the time goes into understanding the problem properly. From there, Teddy builds a focused 6 to 8 week plan with exercises you keep doing between visits.

Island PT is cash-pay, which keeps visits unhurried and one-to-one. In Hawaiʻi you can also start physical therapy without a referral.

At a glance
  • Seen at home, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM
  • One clinician from evaluation to discharge
  • Direct access, no referral needed to begin
  • Virtual consultations available

04 / YOUR THERAPIST

Who you will work with.

Teddy Zabel, PT, DPT, OCS, founded Island PT. He completed his physical therapy doctorate at UNC Chapel Hill, is an ABPTS board-certified Orthopaedic Clinical Specialist, and holds Hawaiʻi physical therapy license PT-4524.

In practice, that means a careful evaluation using tools such as the SFMA, the FMS, and the McKenzie Method, with options including the BackFitPro McGill approach for back pain, Blood Flow Restriction training, and custom-made foot orthotics. Read Teddy's full background.

05 / WHERE WE START

Our three core specialties.

Most plans for Kahului patients draw on one or more of these areas.

Spine & Back Care.

Treatment for back and neck pain that flares with work, driving, or lifting, aimed at the source rather than the symptom.

Mobility & Balance.

Restoring range of motion and control in stiff hips, knees, and ankles so you can move freely and with confidence.

Strength-Based Therapy.

Targeted strength work to help you return to sport, running, and physically demanding days, and to stay there.
