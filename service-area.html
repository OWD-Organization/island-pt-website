02 / AREAS WE SERVE

Communities we care for.

Area 01 · Home Base

Physical Therapy in Kīhei, HI.

Kīhei is home for Island PT. It is where the clinic is based and where most of our in-person sessions happen. The town's active lifestyle, daily ocean swims, paddleboarding, long beach walks, and year-round outdoor living, means people here move a lot and feel the effects when something is off.

We help Kīhei residents with chronic back pain, post-surgical recovery, balance issues, and the everyday wear and tear that comes from staying active in your 40s, 50s, 60s, and beyond. Whether you are a longtime local or recently moved to the south side, we are here to help you keep doing the things you love.

Schedule a Kīhei Visit
Area 02 · South of Kīhei

Physical Therapy in Wailea, HI.

Just south of Kīhei, Wailea is a short drive to the clinic and a community we are proud to serve. From visitors recovering from a sports injury during their stay, to full-time residents managing back pain or planning for surgery recovery, our patients here are looking for one thing: focused, expert care that does not waste their time.

We work with Wailea residents and long-stay visitors on everything from spine and back care to mobility, balance, and post-operative rehab. For those who prefer it, virtual consultations are available as well.

Schedule a Wailea Visit
03 / WHY SOUTH MAUI

A practice close to the people we serve.

South Maui is one of the most active parts of the island.

Between morning paddles off Kīhei, golf at Wailea, hikes up to the lava fields, and a year-round calendar of triathlons and races, people here put real demands on their bodies.

We built Island PT to match. The clinic is positioned to be reachable from across the south side, with appointment availability that respects your time and a treatment model designed to get results in fewer visits, not more.

What that means in practice
  • Centrally located in Kīhei for easy access
  • Short drive from Wailea, Makena, Māʻalaea, and surrounding areas
  • Virtual consultations available for clients beyond the island
  • Focused 6 to 8 week treatment plans, designed to actually finish
