03 / OUR APPROACH

Care that listens. Treatment that lasts.

Our Approach

Pain should never dictate how you live.

Whether you are recovering from surgery, managing long-standing back pain, or simply wanting to move more comfortably as you get older, we build a treatment plan tailored to your body, your story, and your goals.

Every session is one-to-one with your therapist. That means no double-booking, no shuffling between patients, no handing your rehab off to a tech. Just attentive, focused care designed to help you feel better and stay better.

We are here to help if you are dealing with
  • Persistent lower back pain or stiffness
  • Reduced mobility or balance concerns
  • Recovery after surgery or an injury
  • Injuries that always seem to come back

04 / SPECIALTIES

Three focused areas of expert care.

Every specialty at Island PT is built around the same principle: attentive, hands-on treatment that delivers lasting results.

01 · Spine & Back Care

Move with confidence, again.

Evidence-based treatment for chronic back pain, sciatica, disc issues, and postural concerns. We help you understand your pain, gain control, avoid surgery, and move with confidence again.

02 · Mobility & Balance

Range, stability, independence.

Structured rehabilitation that restores your range of motion, builds stability, and lowers your risk of falls. Especially valuable for post-surgery recovery, gait training, and joint care.

03 · Strength-Based Therapy

Movement is medicine.

We believe movement is medicine. Through gentle, progressive strengthening, we keep your body capable and independent, at any age, at any starting point.
